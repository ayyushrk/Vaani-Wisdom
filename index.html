About Vaani

Preserving and passing down timeless repositories of classical Sanskrit wisdom for the modern digital era.

Vaani is an open digital library created to archive poetic philosophical treasures, including classic Subhashitas, Upanishadic prayers, and verses from the Bhagavad Gita. By providing clean english transliterations, accessible translations, and smart topical filtering tags.
